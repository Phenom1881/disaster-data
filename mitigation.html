About this data

Figures come from OpenFEMA's Hazard Mitigation Assistance Projects, every funded subgrant carrying a federal share obligated. "Federal share obligated" is the federal government's committed portion, not the full project cost and not necessarily the amount finally spent. Hazard Mitigation Assistance spans several programs: the Hazard Mitigation Grant Program after a disaster, and Flood Mitigation Assistance, Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities, and the earlier Pre-Disaster Mitigation program before one.

Every jurisdiction's own mitigation projects are listed on its Local page, alongside the disasters, recovery funding, and individual assistance for that place.
