Reading the rankings

10Western states where fire ranks first

From California to Montana, wildfire is the single most-declared hazard across the West. Fire declarations also clear fastest — often approved in days, not weeks — because the fire-management track is built for speed.

33%Illinois' denial rate — the highest of any active state

Being hit often and being approved are not the same thing. Illinois requests frequently but is turned down on roughly a third of them, while Nevada and Colorado are almost never denied.

106Florida declarations — hit hard, and scrutinized

Hurricane states carry both ends of the burden: Florida and Louisiana rank among the most-declared and the most-denied, a sign of how often coastal requests push the edge of eligibility.
