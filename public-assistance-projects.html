About this data

Figures come from OpenFEMA's Public Assistance Funded Projects Details, every funded project with a declaration date in 2000 or later. "Federal share obligated" is the federal government's committed portion — not the full project cost, and not necessarily the amount finally spent. One disaster can fund tens of thousands of separate projects across many applicants.

Disaster Data · A community project. Public Assistance figures from OpenFEMA, refreshed weekly. Federal share obligated reflects committed funding and may be revised as projects close out. Not affiliated with FEMA or DHS.
