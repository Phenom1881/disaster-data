DisasterData.IO builds individual profiles for counties, county-equivalents, independent cities, and tribal jurisdictions. Each generated profile brings together federal declaration history, assistance and mitigation records, and county-level risk and vulnerability context where matching CDC SVI and FEMA NRI data are available.
