Illinois: Weather Emergency Declarations

Original state weather-emergency declarations, compared with federal FEMA declarations and nearby NOAA/NWS Storm Events evidence.

Coverage: 2000-present numbered EO archive; disaster proclamations from 2023 on from the Governor's listing, plus proclamations added by hand: 2006-2011 (Blagojevich, Quinn) from the Governor's releases, Illinois delegation releases and dated news coverage, 2013-2022 (Quinn, Rauner, Pritzker) from IEMA news releases and news coverage, and the August 2024 proclamation from IEMA-OHS; other pre-2023 proclamations are not listed. Proclamations signed weeks after the storm they declare are compared on the storm's first day.
